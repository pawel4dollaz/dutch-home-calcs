What this result does not prove

It does not prove that a particular BCRG declaration is accepted by the current attested software; it does not reproduce every NTA 8800 table, default, monthly climate value, evidence rule, installation sub-system, or rounding convention; and it cannot replace an EP-adviser registration. Use the calculation trace to identify exactly what should be checked against the advisor's software.
