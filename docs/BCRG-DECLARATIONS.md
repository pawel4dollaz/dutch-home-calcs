# BCRG product references used by the screening presets

## Vincent V45-Combi + WPV 150/200 L

- BCRG declaration: **20220186GG** (replacement of 20220035GG)
- Registration date: 2022-05-10
- Declaration PDF: https://mijn.bcrg.nl/media/20220186GK_gelijkwaardigheidsverklaring_Vincent_V45-Combi__06-05-22.pdf
- The declaration states NTA 8800:2020+A1:2020.
- It declares space-heating generation efficiency as a function of heat demand, annual heat demand and design supply temperature.
- WPV 150L DHW ηprac values in the declaration include 2.690 for M and 2.432 for L.

The calculator's Vincent preset uses **4.35** only as a deliberately visible screening point corresponding to the low-temperature/low-energy part of the published space-heating table. It is not a substitute for the declaration's interpolation logic.

## WeHeat Flint P40

- BCRG space-heating declaration: **20240324GK**
- NTA version named in the declaration: NTA 8800:2024
- Declaration date: 2024-11-08
- Space-heating screening point used here: **η = 5.909** at low energy demand and θsup ≤30°C.
- BCRG Flint + WBL-200 DHW declaration: **20250226GK**, dated 2025-08-06.
- DHW screening value used here: **η = 3.026** (M condition).

Again, these presets are intended to make the product comparison explicit. They do not establish current declaration applicability for a new official label.
